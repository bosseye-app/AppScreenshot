# App Store Screenshot Maker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

## Overview

App Store Screenshot Maker is a fully offline macOS app for composing App Store screenshots. We do not collect, upload or share any personal information.

## We collect nothing

- No account, no sign-in.
- No server: the app never sends data to us or to anyone else.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- The app does not request network access.

## File access

- The app can only read images and project files that you pick yourself in the system file dialog.
- It remembers the paths (security-scoped bookmarks) of files you selected, on your Mac only, so they can be restored next time.
- You can remove those records at any time from inside the app.

## Where your data lives

- Your design settings are stored in the local macOS preferences of this app.
- Imported images are used in memory only; exported images are written to the folder you choose.
- Deleting the app removes all of this local data.

## Purchases

- Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store.
- We never receive or store your payment details. The app only asks the system whether a purchase exists, in order to unlock features.

## Children

- The app collects no data and contains no content aimed at children.

## Changes

- If this policy changes, this document and the date above will be updated.

## Contact

- If you have questions, please reach out through the App Support entry on the App Store product page.
